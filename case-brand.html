Brand Concept

I used a U.S. customer survey to shape a playful brand identity, then built a guide for applying it across stores.

Identity · Mascot · Pattern · Merch

01Research

I asked 98 people first

Before drawing anything, I ran a survey across the US. Each question showed two images side by side and asked people how each one felt, from the store interior to packaging and treats.

The answers pointed one way, whether people drink boba once a week or every day: they want everyday treats that feel a little special, and look good enough to share.

  • What they see online shapes what they choose.
  • Feeling at home with a brand is what keeps them coming back.

02The idea

Fast for us, chill for you

The hero is your time. The enemy is daily chaos.

Behind the counter we move fast and precise, so those minutes go back to your day. In front of it, you slow down. The brand speaks like a reliable, energetic friend: quick and smart when you’re busy, warm and down to earth when you want to unwind.

03Brand values

Smart, premium, cozy, cool

04Identity

A logo that swirls like milk in tea

A circular logotype for key moments and a combination mark for logo walls and merch, each in red and warm milk.

05Mascot

Meet ST-X

Born from fluid contours and circular shadows, ST-X slips through any round portal just to ditch the daily rush: your partner in crime for taking a break. A 2D retro explorer for stories and merch, a 3D fluid form for a surreal, chill mood.

07In the world

From cup to street

The system scales from a cup in your hand to cooler bags, gift cards, merch and the storefront.

Reflection

Designing the mascot and patterns was only part of the work. The guide turned those choices into rules that store teams and other designers could apply.

I contributed to the brand’s research, design, planning, and in-store rollout. The company adapted the direction to its commercial priorities. I reworked selected elements for this case study to show my original design intent and visual style.
